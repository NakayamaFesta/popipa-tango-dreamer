## 参考翻译

（英文翻译，来源：animeliryca.com）

What are you doing now?
Why are you worried?
I'm tired of searching for a single answer.

They are priceless, this moment and the future,
Our distant dreams and the glittering past.
All of this was born, because we met.
So let's embrace our present.

Do, do, do what you love, and besides
Love what you do, goes without saying.
Is that the right answer?
Let's worry and at the same time smile.

Pretending to simply bring my face closer,
I glance at your profile and I feel calm.
Is that strange?
Somewhere we'll stumble upon it by chance.
It seems to me that our perfect answer
Has already appeared…

What should we do next?
Where should we go from here?
Even now, when we worry, we live our precious days.

Before we know it, we'll be adults.
When you looked back, I started moving toward you faster.
Wait a little,
I'll catch up with you soon.
So decide to love our present even more.

Do, do, do what you love or love.
(Love, love what you do)
Do, do, do what you love or love.
(Love, love what you do)

Do, do, do what you love, and therefore
Love what you do and move forward like this.
Is that the right answer too?
Let's walk forward and at the same time worry
With our steps of varying widths.

When your hair sways, I feel joy.
Is that strange?
Someday it will be connected.
It seems to me that we've touched
Upon our perfect brilliant answer…

I'm quietly praying, of course, that we,
I'm quietly praying, will have the perfect answer.
